Tribute to A.P.J Abdul Kalam

About A.P.J Abdul Kalam

A.P.J Abdul Kalam the "Missile Man Of India", was born on october 15 1951,in rameswaram,Tamil Nadu.He served as the 11th president of india and was a renowned scientist.

Achievements

  • Developed indias first satellite (SLV-111)
  • Wrote book like "wings of fire" and "india 2020"
  • Reserved the bharat ratna
  • Led indias successful nuclear test in 1998
